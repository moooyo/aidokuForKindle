**Stock process bounds.** The [stock report and its failed visual gate](../startup-native-20261121/README.md#first-complete-batch-visual-check-failed) are separate from a release-to-SEND measurement. On `test-env`, the `native_tile_report` entry in `aidoku-startup-evidence-20261121.json` in the coordination directory identifies the case brackets. Join each timed case to its `anchor` archive member and the corresponding `discard-case`/`birth_ticks` entry in the closed batch's `receipt.json`. Apply the [100 Hz tick and interval arithmetic](../startup-native-20261121/analyze_birth.py#L16). The six pairs are **diagnostic only**: both final right toolbars differed from the frozen reference. The later repeat stopped before the first click and supplied no replacement data.

**Staged application timings.** Use the same worker index's `independent_staged_screens` → `warm-*/attempt-*/output.tar.gz`; verify `operations_passed` and the matching release in `operations.json`. Read `out/{baseline,candidate}-01` through `-06`, not warmups or pictures. Each `shell.tsv` has only `staged_foreground_requested`, `launcher_worker` and `stock_returned`. In `application.json`, require `process_entry.at_us == 0` and the [complete, same-frame Library trace checks](../startup-followup-20261121/rendered-library/replay.py#L32). Rust-to-Library is `startup_library_frame_submitted.at_us`. Estimate Rust entry on the boot clock as `startup_initial_frame_submitted.boottime_us - startup_initial_frame_submitted.at_us`; subtract the launcher mark converted from 10 ms uptime bins. The [published six pairs](../startup-followup-20261121/rendered-library/staged-warm-post-send.json) used the earlier staged-request anchor and are **not ordinary-card visits**.

**Historical launcher detail.** Take the six `records` in the [Nov 17 per-visit export](../startup-catalog-optimization-20261117/evidence/remaining-opportunity-evidence.json) and the [marker definitions](../startup-catalog-optimization-20261117/analyze-remaining.py#L109). The portrait helper's internal median was 161.648 ms; the median of **per-visit** outer minus inner was 114.828 ms, including scheduling, supervision and process lifetime, not a removable wait. A separate [R3 indexed replay](../startup-entry-timing-20261119/r3-replay.json) recorded 280 ms outer portrait and 100 ms takeover on warm opens. Neither study measures residual observer wait on the later overlapping launcher. The later [ten-marker launcher instrument](../startup-followup-20261121/DIAGNOSTICS.md#warm-opens-separate-observer-wait-portrait-and-display-takeover) had only host fixtures and preflight, not a closed hardware timing visit.

**Why the JPEG cohort cannot fill the gaps.** All 14 returned `case.tar.gz` files (two warmups and six pairs) contained exactly `origin`, `anchor`, `pointer-brackets`, `qualified`, `submission`. The [frozen parser](../startup-jpeg-native-20261122/host.py#L118) and [cohort result](../startup-jpeg-native-20261122/README.md#scribe-result-no-consistent-first-screen-improvement) bind ordinary release to successful Library submission, but retain neither process birth, Rust entry nor launcher-stage timestamps. Its consistency rule failed; the timing data are valid for their original endpoint, not evidence of which stage changed. A missing stage is unknown, not zero.
