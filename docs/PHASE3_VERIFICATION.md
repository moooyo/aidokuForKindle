## Scope and limitations

Automatic brightness is not implemented or qualified. The lighting review accepts
the implemented exposed frontlight/warmth controls, with the absent reference
auto rows and panel-height differences preserved in the
[capability disposition](review/phase3-c16-final-design-pass2-light-scope-adjudication-01.json).
This does not establish that Scribe hardware lacks automatic brightness or grant
unconditional design parity. Unconfigured tracking, unavailable controls and
unobserved operations are not accepted as working integrations.

The dedicated firmware VM used synthetic sensor input and RAM fixtures after
guest persistent-storage failures. Those conditions are explicit software
verification limits, not physical Kindle, persistent-filesystem, waveform, RF,
frontlight or physical input/display-latency acceptance. Up/Down presentation
retains chapter/page and manual 90/270 preferences; manual four-way choices are
not a claim of automatic four-way rotation.

Closed Reader shows source artwork without persistent app page/footer/ribbon,
bookmark corner, status/debug or hidden footer-cycle controls. Its source borders
and printed marks are distinguished using its own processed/expected inputs.

Independent test-env work may run in isolated workspaces, outputs and targets;
only genuinely shared writable resources require a mutex. The Native owner
controls its dedicated VM/QMP and checked measurement pause/restore. The physical
Kindle lease was not acquired or modified. No local Windows validation was run.
